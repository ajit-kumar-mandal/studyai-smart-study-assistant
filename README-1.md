## 👋 A little about me

I'm **Ajit Kumar Mandal**, founder of **[Study AI](https://mystudyai.online)** — an AI-powered product built to move beyond one-shot chatbot interactions.

My work centres on shaping the product experience and the architecture around it: connecting AI models with tools, designing multi-step execution flows, integrating services, and building features that help people get useful work done.

I’m an independent, product-focused builder from **Bihar, India 🇮🇳**. I learn by researching, prototyping, testing ideas, and improving a real product. I’m especially interested in the intersection of **Agentic AI, systems thinking, product architecture, and practical automation**.

> **My approach:** Don't stop at generating an answer. Design the system that can take the next useful step.

## 🚀 What I'm building

### [Study AI](https://mystudyai.online) — AI that goes beyond conversation

Study AI brings conversational experiences together with specialised workflows and action-oriented AI capabilities.

Areas represented in the project include:

- **Agent runtime:** iterative tool calling, tool-result feedback, bounded execution, retries, and task continuation.
- **Tool & app integrations:** catalog-based tool discovery, MCP-oriented integrations, and application capability resolution.
- **Model orchestration:** provider routing and fallback paths for different AI execution needs.
- **Study workflows:** AI notes, quizzes, live quiz rooms, study planning, and homework assistance.
- **Voice experiences:** streaming and realtime voice-related product infrastructure.
- **Web and Android:** a React + TypeScript application with a Capacitor-based Android build workflow.

The goal is to make AI more useful in everyday workflows — not just more conversational.
